Engineering Standard

The 10⁵–10⁸ Ω
Controlled Zone

Too conductive and charge dissipates too fast — causing CDM failures. Too insulative and charge builds up silently on your tooling. EGK's entire semiconductor coating portfolio is engineered to land and stay in the controlled dissipation zone.
